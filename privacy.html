Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

MaiWebStudio is a web design business based in Birmingham, UK. If you have any questions about your data, email mtechsecure81@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send the booking form, we receive the details you type in:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number and business name, if you add them
  • the package, template and price estimate you picked, and your message

Why we use it

Only to reply to you, send you a project brief, arrange a call and, if you go ahead, build your website. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract. We never sell your details or add you to a mailing list.

Where it’s stored

Form messages are stored in our Google account (Google Sheets and Gmail). Google may store data outside the UK under its standard data protection terms.

How long we keep it

If you don’t become a client, we delete your details within 12 months. If you do, we keep project records for as long as we need them for the work and for our accounts.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time by emailing us. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. It remembers whether you picked light or dark mode in your own browser, and loads fonts from Google Fonts.
