Terms

Last updated 24 November 2026. These terms apply when you book and pay for a website from MaiWebStudio (run by Mai, a sole trader based in Birmingham).

1. What you’re buying

  • Template website: one of our ready-made designs, set up with your logo, colours, text, photos and contact details, plus any extras you chose in the price calculator.
  • Custom website: a site designed around your brand. We agree the final scope and price on an online design consultation before building starts.
  • Anything not included in your order is extra, and we’ll always quote before doing it.

2. Paying

  • Payments are taken securely by card through Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Templates are paid in full when you book.
  • Custom websites need a 50% deposit to secure your slot. The balance is due before your site goes live. If the agreed scope changes the price, we’ll confirm the new total in writing first.
  • Maintenance & hosting (£50/month) is set up separately when your site launches, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice.

3. What we need from you

  • Fill in the brief we email you, and send your logo, photos and text.
  • You confirm you own, or have permission to use, everything you send us.
  • If we wait more than 30 days for your content, we may pause your project and the timeline will move.

4. Timeline and changes

  • Template sites are usually ready to review about 7 working days after we have your brief and content. We’ll give you a date for custom sites at the consultation.
  • Two rounds of changes are included. Extra changes are quoted first.

5. Cancelling and refunds

  • If you cancel before we start work, we’ll refund you in full.
  • Once work has started, payments cover the work already done and aren’t refundable. If we can’t complete your site for a reason on our side, we’ll refund anything you’ve paid for work we haven’t done.

Buying for yourself, not a business? Under UK consumer law you have 14 days to cancel. By booking, you ask us to start work within those 14 days. If you cancel during that time, you’ll get a refund minus the cost of any work already done. To cancel, email mtechsecure81@gmail.com with your order number.

  • We may show your site in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.
  • Code and tools we reuse across clients stay ours, and you get a permanent licence to use them on your site.

7. Responsibility

  • We’re not responsible for problems caused by third-party services (for example hosting, Google, Shopify or payment providers), or changes made by someone else.
